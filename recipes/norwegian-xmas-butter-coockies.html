Norwegian Christmas Butter Squares

Also known as Sugar Cookie Bars — rich, buttery, and chewy bar cookies.

Prep: 10 mins
Bake: 12–15 mins
Oven: 400°F (200°C)
Pan: 9x13-inch

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (plus a little extra if the dough is overly soft)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Turbinado sugar, raw sugar, or white sugar (for topping/dusting)

Instructions

  1. Prep Pan & Oven: Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Chill an ungreased 9x13-inch baking pan in the freezer while preparing the dough. Do not grease the pan.
  2. Cream Wet Ingredients: Using a mixer, blend the softened butter, egg, granulated sugar, and salt together until smooth and creamy.
  3. Mix Dough: Add the flour and vanilla extract. Use your hands to mix until the dough comes together in large clumps. If it feels overly soft, work in a little extra flour.
  4. Press & Dust: Press the dough evenly onto the chilled, ungreased baking pan until it is roughly ¼-inch thick. Generously dust the entire top surface with sugar (as much as the dough will hold).
  5. Bake: Bake at 400°F for 12 to 15 minutes, until the edges turn a light golden brown.
  6. Slice & Serve: Remove from the oven and let cool for about 5 minutes. Cut the warm baked dough into squares, then gently lift them out of the pan using a spatula.
Tip: These pair wonderfully with a hot cup of bitter coffee or tea!
