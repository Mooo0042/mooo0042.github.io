🎸 Clone Hero Chart Converter

Convert .chart files to 5 timestamp lists (Green, Red, Yellow, Blue, Orange)

📋 How to use:

  • Drag and drop your .chart file or click to browse
  • Only N (note) events are processed - ignores S (star power) and E (events)
  • Long presses (notes with duration > 0) are encoded as single timestamps at start time
  • Timestamps are in milliseconds from song start
  • Export as individual text files (one per fret) or combined formats
📝 Note: This converter uses BPM data from [SyncTrack] to accurately convert chart ticks to real-time milliseconds.
